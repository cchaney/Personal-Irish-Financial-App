# Disclaimer

**PIFA is not financial, investment, tax or pension advice.**

PIFA is a personal hobby project provided free under the MIT licence, "as is", with no warranty of any kind.

- **Not security audited.** PIFA is designed to run on your own computer only. It has no user accounts by default, has not been independently security tested, and must not be hosted on a server or made reachable from the internet.
- **Not a regulated service.** PIFA, its authors and contributors are not authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland or any other regulator to give financial, investment, pension or tax advice. Nothing PIFA shows you, including the AI coach, insights and simulator results, is a personal recommendation.
- **Projections are estimates.** The simulator uses assumed returns and random market scenarios. Real returns, fees, inflation and tax will differ, and investments can lose value. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future performance.
- **Tax rules change.** Irish tax, pension and social welfare figures in PIFA are for 2026 (see `backend/app/services/irish_tax.py`) and were believed accurate when written. They change at least every Budget. Always check [revenue.ie](https://www.revenue.ie), [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie) and [gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie).
- **AI can be wrong.** AI-generated answers can be inaccurate or incomplete. Verify anything important.
- **Your data is your responsibility.** PIFA stores data locally in a file you control. Keep backups, keep your computer secure, and don't expose PIFA to the internet. If you use the optional Anthropic (Claude) AI provider, a summary of your finances is sent to Anthropic's API under their terms.
- **Third-party data.** Optional price updates come from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints, which are unofficial, may break or be inaccurate, and are subject to Yahoo's terms. PIFA isn't affiliated with any bank, pension provider, broker or data provider named in the app.

For decisions about pensions, mortgages, large investments, pension transfers or retirement income, speak to a **Qualified Financial Adviser (QFA)** or **Certified Financial Planner (CFP)**, ideally one paid by fee rather than commission. You can check whether a firm is authorised on the [Central Bank of Ireland register](https://registers.centralbank.ie).
